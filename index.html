Why the White Butter

Not mythology
recreated.
Emotion reimagined.

"In Krishna's childhood, makhan was never just food.
It was innocence. Purity. Warmth. Unconditional love."

Brijwasis would churn butter through the night — not to store it, but secretly hoping Krishna would come and steal it. In Vrindavan, even stolen butter felt sacred. From that timeless feeling, The White Butter was imagined.
